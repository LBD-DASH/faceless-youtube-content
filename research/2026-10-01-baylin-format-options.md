### 1.2 Made-for-kids (MFK) limits, checked 1 Oct 2026

Source: YouTube Help, "Set your channel or video's audience", https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9527654

- **No personalised ads** on MFK content. YouTube itself says this "may result in a decrease in revenue".
- Turned off on MFK videos: comments, cards and end screens, notification bell, channel memberships, Super Chat and Super Stickers, merchandise, Miniplayer playback, "Save to playlist" and "Save to watch later", autoplay on home.
- YouTube says MFK videos are "more likely to be recommended alongside other kids' videos".
- Practical effect: no end screens means a Short or song cannot formally link a viewer to an episode, and no bell means subscribers are not notified. Discovery leans on search, suggested videos and channel playlists.

**12 to 24 months**, possibly longer. Aspetat-level performance (30K to 90K views per new story) is reachable only after a catalogue builds up. Add about 1 month for YPP review and time to reach the AdSense payout threshold.

### 3.2 Estimated RPM (ESTIMATE)
**$0.20 to $1.20 per 1,000 views**, with much higher revenue PER VIEW than Option 1 because one view can run for 20 to 60 minutes and carry several mid-rolls. Big caveat: YouTube says meditation-style videos "may not be suitable for mid-roll ads" (Help 6175006), and top competitors advertise "No Ads" (Koala Moon) or "No mid-roll ads" (Cosmic Kids, per a kidvideo.org listing of their 2 hour compilation, https://kidvideo.org/video/bedtime-relaxation-stories-for-kids-sleep-adventures-2-hour-compilation-cosmic-kids-i108981.html). If Kevin chooses few or no mid-rolls to protect sleep, revenue per view drops toward Option 1 levels; watch hours still accrue.

### 3.3 Production effort per video (ESTIMATE)
**3 to 6 hours per compilation per language**, once 3 or more episodes exist in that language (stitch episodes, record a short new welcome and goodnight bookend with the narrator, 1 or 2 new calm transition drawings, licensed soft music from the YouTube Audio Library, chapters, loudness check, thumbnail). About 9 to 18 hours for all three languages. A pure loop of one episode would take under 2 hours but carries the policy risk below.

### 3.4 Estimated time to first revenue (ESTIMATE)
**6 to 15 months after the first compilation goes live**, so roughly month 8 to 18 from today. Reasoning: assuming 15 to 30 minutes average view duration (estimate, parents often leave sleep videos running), 8,000 hours needs roughly 16K to 32K views, far fewer than Option 1. The 1,000 subscriber requirement then becomes the main bottleneck.

### 3.6 Risks
- **Reused content policy:** compilations are explicitly reviewed under it ("There is no change to our reused content policy which reviews content like commentary, clips, compilations, and reaction videos"), and it applies "to your channel as a whole" (YouTube channel monetization policies, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392). Reusing Kevin's own original episodes is lower risk than reusing others' work, but the channel must clearly show it made the content.
- **Inauthentic (repetitive or mass-produced) content policy:** not allowed to monetise includes "Image slideshows, templated storylines, or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative" and content that "feels interchangeable from video to video" (same source; policy renamed from "repetitious content" on 15 Jul 2025). A channel dominated by near-identical loops is the main way to fail YPP review. Mitigation: each compilation should have a distinct set of episodes, new bookend narration and distinct visuals, and should stay a minority of uploads.
- **Ads vs sleep:** mid-rolls can wake children; competitors sell "no ads" as a feature.
- **Dependency:** needs a back catalogue. With only Episodes 1 to 3 approved, a strong 1 hour compilation per language realistically needs Episode 4 or more (or longer later episodes).
- **Music:** only use music with a clear licence (for example the YouTube Audio Library) to avoid Content ID claims across a 1 hour file.

## 6. Recommendation

**Use a mix, led by episodes:**

1. **Base: keep 8 to 10 minute episodes as the core format** in all three languages, starting with approved Episodes 1 to 3. They prove originality for YPP review, can carry a mid-roll, and supply the raw material for everything else.
2. **Compilations from Episode 4 onward:** once 4 or more episodes exist in a language, publish one 1 hour compilation per language, then roughly one new compilation for every 3 to 4 new episodes. Each gets new bookend narration, distinct visuals and licensed soft music, never a raw loop. Keep compilations a minority of uploads, and test mid-roll settings carefully (few, at story breaks) because sleep and ads conflict.
3. **Shorts as light discovery only:** 1 or 2 Shorts per episode, capped at about 1 hour of work each, not counted on for revenue. Prefer purpose-made vertical moments over plain clips, since plain story clips underperformed on comparable channels.
4. **Songs and lullabies: park for now.** Revisit after YPP, and only if the content team wants to write lyrics; then test one lullaby per language.
5. **Plan against the 8,000 hour threshold** that applies to new applicants from 1 Feb 2027, and judge progress by watch hours and subscribers, not Shorts views.
