## Full voiceover script

Narration only below. Sound cues in [brackets]. Visual notes sit under each block. Speak slowly, warmly, like a bedtime storyteller. Pause where marked. Target: about 1000 to 1250 words of narration.

### [0:00-0:20] Hook

[soft night breeze; very quiet room ambience]

Tonight, over the Kingdom of Sunhill, the moon is late to rise... and Princess Baylin is about to learn that worry cannot make it come any faster.

Come with us into a dark bedroom window, up a quiet hillside, and into a lesson that grows as slowly and kindly as the night itself.

**Visual:** Title card fades in over a soft navy sky with no moon yet. Warm gold window glow below. Calm faces.

---

### [0:20-1:20] Beat 1 - Wide awake

[soft bedsheets rustle; distant hush of night]

Once upon a soft night, when the palace was quiet, Princess Baylin lay tucked under her blanket. Everything was ready for sleep.

But Baylin's eyes were wide open.

She looked at her window. The curtains were open on a flat, starless navy sky. No moon. No little silver friends. Only dark.

"Where is the moon?" she whispered. "It always comes to say goodnight."

She sat up in bed. Waiting alone felt very long when you are small and the sky stays empty.

**Visual:** Baylin sitting up in bed, blankets around her waist. Curtains open on a starless navy sky. Soft warm lamp or window frame gold against cool blue.

---

### [1:20-2:10] Beat 2 - Tilly notices

[slow tortoise shuffle at the door; no creak scare]

A gentle tap came at the door. Then Tilly the tortoise poked her head in, one careful step at a time. Tortoises notice everything, even the quiet things.

"Still awake?" asked Tilly softly.

Baylin nodded. "Look at the floor, Tilly. Usually the moon makes a silver puddle right there. Tonight there is nothing."

Tilly blinked slowly. The floor was dark. The moonlight that usually pooled by the bed was missing.

"Hmm," said Tilly. "The night is taking its time."

Baylin frowned a little. "I do not like it when things take their time."

**Visual:** Tilly at the bedroom door. Floor by the bed is dark where moonlight should be. Two-shot, calm and quiet.

---

### [2:10-3:00] Beat 3 - A moon to find

[cloak fabric soft swish; determined little footsteps]

Baylin slipped out of bed. She pulled a soft cloak over her nightgown and tied it carefully under her chin.

"The moon must be lost," she said. "Or stuck. Or forgotten the way to Sunhill. I will go and find it myself."

Tilly's eyes widened, slow as sunrise. "At night? Over the hill?"

"Yes," said Baylin, trying to sound brave. "Worrying in bed will not bring the moon any faster. Fetching it might."

She did not yet know that fetching was not the answer. But her heart was kind.

**Visual:** Baylin in cloak over nightgown, determined but soft. Tilly looking up. Warm room light against dark window.

---

### [3:00-3:50] Beat 4 - Too fast on the hill

[hurried soft footsteps on dirt; small stumble; no hurt cry]

They left the palace and climbed the hill outside the castle. The path was moonless and shadowy. Baylin walked too quickly. Her cloak flapped. Her feet skipped roots she normally noticed.

Then... [soft stumble]

Baylin tripped over a root and sat down with a little bump. She was not hurt. Only surprised, and a tiny bit cross with the dark.

"Ow. The hill is being unhelpful," she said.

Tilly arrived behind her, one steady step after another. "The hill is the same as always," said Tilly. "We are the ones in a hurry."

Baylin rubbed her knee and sighed. "I just want the moon."

**Visual:** Moonless hillside path. Baylin stumbling gently, then sitting. Tilly plodding steadily behind. Soft navy and shadow, no scary shapes.

---

### [3:50-4:40] Beat 5 - The owl speaks

[soft owl hoot, low and friendly; leaves barely stir]

From a bare branch above them came a calm voice.

"Hoo. Looking for the moon, little princess?"

Baylin looked up. There sat a round owl with big amber eyes. The owl did not look scary. It looked like a soft night lamp with feathers.

"Yes," said Baylin. "It is late. Can you tell us where it went?"

The owl tilted its head kindly. "The moon rises when it is ready, not when it is called. Worry cannot pull it up. Only time can."

Baylin crossed her arms. She was not yet ready to believe it.

**Visual:** Calm round owl on a bare branch against dark sky. Amber eyes soft, not glaring. Baylin and Tilly looking up.

---

### [4:40-5:20] Beat 6 - Calling the moon

[Baylin calls upward; empty echo of open night; no answer]

Baylin cupped her hands around her mouth and called as loudly as she could into the dark sky.

"Moon! Moon! Please hurry! We are waiting in Sunhill!"

She called again, louder, the way she once sang too loudly for the rain.

And then... [soft empty night hush]

Nothing happened. No moon. No silver. Only the quiet dark.

Baylin's cheeks felt warm. She was frustrated, but she was still safe, and Tilly was still beside her.

**Visual:** Baylin shouting upward, cheeks a little puffed. Empty sky. Soft comic beat, never distress. Echo of Episode 1 shout-first instinct without copying the garden.

---

### [5:20-6:00] Beat 7 - Sit and wait

[pause; soft breeze; tortoise shell settling on stone]

Tilly found a flat stone and settled onto it with a soft little shell sound.

"Perhaps," said Tilly, "we stop trying to fetch it. Perhaps we sit and wait together."

She patted the space beside her with one careful foot.

Baylin hesitated. Sitting felt like doing nothing. Doing nothing felt hard when you want something badly.

Then she looked at Tilly's calm face, and she sat down. The stone was cool. The air was soft. The hurry in her feet began to quiet, just a little.

"We will wait," Baylin said. "But I am not good at waiting."

"That is all right," said Tilly. "Waiting is a skill you practise. Like walking slowly. Like listening."

**Visual:** Tilly on flat stone, patting space beside her. Baylin sitting. Two small figures under a wide dark sky.

---

### [6:00-6:50] Beat 8 - Counting stars (child pause)

[very soft twinkle chimes, one by one; sparse]

They sat side by side and looked up.

"Look," whispered Tilly. "The first star."

A tiny light blinked on in the navy sky.

"One," said Baylin.

Another blinked on.

"Two."

Then a third, faint as a pinprick of silver.

"Three."

[On-screen cue: "Count with us… how many stars?"]

[3-5 second pause for the child to count along]

Can you count them too? One. Two. Three. Four. Five. Soft little lights, arriving when they are ready.

Baylin's shoulders lowered. Counting made the waiting feel like a gentle game.

**Visual:** Close on the sky. Stars blink on one at a time. Optional soft number overlays (EN; AF/ZU only after native check). Mid-story child pause.

---

### [6:50-7:30] Beat 9 - Did the moon forget?

[music thins; still calm; soft wind]

After a while Baylin hugged her knees.

"What if the moon forgot about Sunhill altogether?" she asked.

Tilly stayed steady beside her.

"The moon has not forgotten," said Tilly. "It is only sleepy. Sleepy things take longer to wake. That does not mean they are gone."

Baylin watched the dark horizon. Doubt sat with her for a moment, then Tilly's words sat with her too.

"I will try to trust it," Baylin whispered.

**Visual:** Baylin hugging knees. Tilly calm and close. Horizon still dark. Warm emotional beat, no tears bait.

---

### [7:30-8:20] Beat 10 - A silver glow

[soft rising pad; night colours warm slightly]

Then something changed.

At the edge of the world, where the hill met the sky, a faint silver glow began.

"Tilly," breathed Baylin. "Do you see that?"

"I see it," said Tilly.

The glow grew, slowly, kindly. Navy softened toward silver. The stones on the hill found gentle edges.

She did not jump up. She did not shout. She watched, the way you watch a flower open when you have finally stopped poking it.

**Visual:** Horizon lightening from navy to soft silver over several beats. Slow pan. Faces lit from below by growing glow.

---

### [8:20-9:10] Beat 11 - The moon rises

[warm soft swell; gentle night music; no fanfare crash]

And then the full moon rose over the Kingdom of Sunhill.

It was round and calm and bright enough to make the hillside soft. Shadows stepped back. The owl's amber eyes glowed like tiny friendly lanterns. Baylin stood up slowly, as if standing too fast might scare the light away.

"It came," she said. "It really came."

She understood then. The answer was never to rush the moon. The answer was to trust it, and wait, and keep a friend beside you while the sky takes its time.

"Thank you for waiting with me," she told Tilly.

"Thank you for sitting," said Tilly. "Sitting is harder than running, sometimes."

**Visual:** Wide shot of full moon over Sunhill. Hillside bathed in soft silver. Baylin and Tilly small and safe. Owl still calm on its branch.

---

### [9:10-10:00] Beat 12 - Home and soft sleep + thank-you + Ep 3 tease

[music slows; soft footsteps home; bedsheets; fade toward hush]

They walked home calmly in the moonlight. No hurry now.

Back in her room, moonlight made a soft puddle on the floor, exactly where it belonged. Baylin climbed into bed. Her eyes felt heavy in the nicest way.

"Thank you, friends!" said Baylin.

[On-screen: "Thank you, friends!" / AF "Dankie, vriende!" / ZU "Siyabonga, bangane!" - AF/ZU NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK]

Then Baylin tipped her face toward the moon at her window and whispered goodnight, the way she always does when a story is done.

What can you wait for patiently today? Perhaps a turn. Waiting is a quiet kind of bravery.

And if you listen very carefully tomorrow night, you may hear another quiet adventure, when Princess Baylin meets the Quiet Star, and learns that small lights can be enough.

Goodnight, little listener. Sleep well. The moon will keep watch softly while you dream.

[fade out]

**Visual:** Warm interior. Moonlight through the window. Baylin's eyes closing. Thank-you ritual card. Optional soft Quiet Star silhouette tease for Episode 3. No hard sell, no end-screen style push.
