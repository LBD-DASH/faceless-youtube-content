## Full voiceover script

Narration only below. Sound cues in [brackets]. Visual notes sit under each block. Speak slowly, warmly, like a bedtime storyteller. Pause where marked. Target: about 1000 to 1250 words of narration.

Narrator (Kevin, 2026-09-30): an old wise man with a warm, deep storytelling tone (not young, not neutral). A dedicated voice for each of English, Afrikaans, and isiZulu. Applies to this episode and all future episodes. No voice IDs assigned.

### [0:00-0:25] Hook

[soft hill breeze; distant birds; slow tortoise shuffle]

Princess Baylin wanted to race to the picnic meadow. Tilly the tortoise walked slowly beside her. "Come on!" Baylin called. But the best part of the day was waiting on the slow path.

Come with us to a late-afternoon hill in the Kingdom of Sunhill, where kindness learns to take its time.

**Visual:** Title card over a warm hill path. Baylin leaning forward. Tilly calm. Soft gold light. Calm faces.

---

### [0:25-1:15] Beat 1 - Picnic plans

[soft basket creak; grass hush; warm afternoon air]

Once upon a late afternoon, when the sun was soft and gold, Princess Baylin packed a small picnic basket at the edge of the Kingdom of Sunhill.

Bread. Berries. A little cloth for sitting. She bounced on her toes.

"Race you to the meadow!" she said to Tilly the tortoise. "We can eat before the sky turns pink."

Tilly smiled her slow smile. "We will get there in the end," she said. "The path does not mind waiting."

Baylin lifted the basket. The meadow felt so close. Her feet were already ready to run.

**Visual:** Castle edge or cottage yard. Small picnic basket. Warm gold light. Baylin eager. Tilly steady. Soft green hill ahead. Late-afternoon palette (new for the series after Ep 4's morning river).

---

### [1:15-2:05] Beat 2 - Race ahead

[quick light footsteps; basket thump; tortoise shuffle fading]

Baylin started down the hill path, steps quick and bright.

"I will carry the basket for both of us," she called. "That will help us go faster!"

Tilly began at her own calm pace. One careful foot. Then another.

Baylin looked back once. Tilly looked small on the path. The meadow looked big and inviting ahead.

"I will save you a place!" Baylin called, and she hurried on.

The basket bumped against her knee. The path blurred a little under her feet.

**Visual:** Baylin hurrying ahead with basket. Tilly smaller behind. Soft comic hurry, never panic. Warm path, no cliffs, no danger.

---

### [2:05-2:55] Beat 3 - Left behind

[breeze; Baylin footsteps thin ahead; slow tortoise pause]

Tilly stopped beside a little wildflower and sniffed it kindly.

"Baylin?" she called, soft as a leaf.

But Baylin was already around the bend, too far to hear.

Tilly looked at the empty stretch of path. She was not angry. Only a little alone.

"We'll get there in the end," she whispered to herself. And she kept walking, slow and true.

Far ahead, Baylin did not know that the nicest part of the day had just been left behind.

**Visual:** Tilly alone by a wildflower. Empty path. Baylin tiny in the distance. Gentle loneliness, never distress-bait. Soft peach-gold light.

---

### [2:55-3:45] Beat 4 - The empty path

[basket set down hard; wind in grass; quiet]

Baylin reached a flat rock that looked like a perfect picnic seat. She set the basket down with a proud little thump.

"I won!" she said to the empty air.

Then she looked around.

No Tilly. No slow smile. No soft "We'll get there in the end."

Just wind in the grass, and a basket that suddenly felt too big for one.

Baylin's bounce went still. The meadow was pretty. But the pretty felt thin without her friend.

**Visual:** Flat rock. Basket alone. Baylin looking back down the empty path. Soft emotional beat, no tears bait.

---

### [3:45-4:35] Beat 5 - Baylin turns back

[soft footsteps reverse; basket lift; calmer pace]

Baylin remembered the river, and how a sorry had needed hands that stayed.

She remembered a moon that would not hurry, and a quiet star that shone soft and small.

"Oh," she whispered. "I raced the path. But I left the friendship behind."

She lifted the basket again. This time she walked back down the hill. Not racing. Looking.

Around the bend she saw a small patterned shell moving slowly through the gold light.

"Tilly!" Baylin called, and her voice was glad and gentle.

**Visual:** Baylin turning back. Warm cheeks. Tilly coming into view. Soft reunion energy without loudness.

---

### [4:35-5:25] Beat 6 - Side by side

[two sets of footsteps, one slow; basket shared; breeze]

Baylin met Tilly halfway. She set the basket down between them.

"I am sorry I ran ahead," Baylin said. "I wanted to help by being fast. But I think I helped wrong."

Tilly's eyes were kind. "Fast can carry a basket," she said. "Slow can carry a friend."

Baylin nodded. "May I walk with you? At your pace?"

"Yes," said Tilly. "That is the best kind of race. The one we finish together."

They started again. Side by side. One careful step matching another.

**Visual:** Side-by-side walk. Basket between them or Baylin matching Tilly's steps. Warm teaching beat, no scolding.

---

### [5:25-6:15] Beat 7 - Slow soft and kind (child pause)

[soft footfall; grass whisper; gentle underscore]

[On-screen cue: "Slow... soft and kind"]

[3-5 second pause for the child to whisper or take one slow breath along]

Baylin matched Tilly's steps. Left. Pause. Right. Pause.

"Slow... soft and kind," Baylin whispered.

Can you walk the slow path with them too? Soft. Kind. One careful step. Kindness that takes its time still arrives.

A little bee drifted past without hurry. The hill seemed friendlier at this pace.

Tilly hummed a tiny tune only slow walkers seem to hear.

**Visual:** Close on matching steps. Mid-story child pause with on-screen cue. Soft gold grass. Bee optional and gentle. Calm faces.

---

### [6:15-7:05] Beat 8 - Gifts on the path

[soft wonder; bird call; breeze in wildflowers]

Because they walked slowly, they saw things Baylin had missed when she raced.

A beetle shining like a tiny green jewel. A wildflower that smelled like honey. A smooth stone shaped almost like a heart.

"I did not see these before," Baylin said.

"They were here," Tilly said. "The fast path hides them. The slow path shows them."

Baylin tucked the heart-stone gently into the basket cloth, not as a prize, just as a thank-you to the day.

**Visual:** Beetle, wildflower, heart-stone. Discovery montage that stays calm, never frantic. Warm late light.

---

### [7:05-7:55] Beat 9 - The meadow arrives

[soft arrival hush; cloth unfold; distant pink sky]

They reached the meadow as the sky began to lean toward pink.

The flat rock was still there. The grass was soft. The picnic tasted better somehow, because both friends were present for every bite.

Baylin laughed a quiet laugh. "We got here in the end."

"We did," said Tilly. "And we brought the path with us."

The berries were sweet. The breeze was kind. The day felt whole.

**Visual:** Meadow arrival. Picnic cloth. Soft pink tipping into evening. Baylin and Tilly together. Warm contentment.

---

### [7:55-8:45] Beat 10 - Tilly's lesson

[warm soft pad; gentle underscore]

Tilly rested near the cloth and looked at Baylin with her steady eyes.

"Kindness takes its time," Tilly said. "A friend who matches your steps is a gift that hurry cannot buy."

Baylin nodded, remembering it the way you remember a song you want to keep.

"I will try the slow path when I walk with you," she said. "And notice what hurry hides."

Tilly's smile was slow and bright. "We'll get there in the end," she said. "Especially when we go together."

**Visual:** Tilly lesson close-up. Baylin nodding. Soft emotional landing. No lecture framing. Peach-gold meadow light.

---

### [8:45-9:25] Beat 11 - Thanks and Bonayo

[soft owl call far away; evening wash; gentle thanks]

As evening came on, a familiar soft call floated from a nearby tree.

"Hoo-hoo."

Bonayo the owl blinked his round wise eyes from a high branch.

"Well listened, Princess Baylin," Bonayo said, warm as old wood.

Baylin pressed her hands together in thanks.

"Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, Bonayo. Thank you, slow path."

Tilly dipped her head. Bonayo settled his feathers. The meadow held the quiet like a soft blanket.

**Visual:** Bonayo in tree (confirmed canon owl). Thanks beat. Evening wash. Keep Bonayo gentle and brief. No scary night framing.

---

### [9:25-10:00] Beat 12 - Home and soft sleep + thank-you + Ep 6 tease

[music slows; soft footsteps home; bedsheets; fade toward hush]

They walked home calmly along the hill path. No race now. Only the memory of matching steps, and kindness that took its time.

Back in her room, Baylin climbed into bed. Her eyes felt heavy in the nicest way. Outside the window, the hill kept its quiet gold for a little longer.

"Thank you, friends!" said Baylin.

[On-screen: "Thank you, friends!" / AF "Dankie, vriende!" / ZU "Siyabonga, bangane!" - AF/ZU NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK]

Then Baylin tipped her face toward the soft dark and whispered goodnight, the way she always does when a story is done.

What slow kindness could you try today? Perhaps walking with someone. Perhaps noticing one small thing hurry usually hides.

And if you listen very carefully on another calm day, you may hear a gentle story called Princess Baylin and the Market of Small Kindnesses, when sharing makes a whole day brighter.

Goodnight, little listener. Sleep well. The patient hill will keep a soft watch while you dream.

[fade out]

**Visual:** Warm interior. Soft evening through the window. Distant hill silhouette. Baylin's eyes closing. Thank-you ritual card. Optional soft market-stall silhouette tease for a later episode. No hard sell, no end-screen style push.
