# Episode 2 outline: Princess Baylin and the Sleepy Moon

Source: princess-baylin `logs/2026-09-27.md` (cast and tone), plus the cross-project note asking for a night-sky setting and a patience-based resolution. No `handoff/youtube/` file exists yet in princess-baylin, so this is built from the cast and world established for Episode 1 (Tilly the tortoise, the Kingdom of Sunhill), carried forward as placeholders pending Kevin's canon approval.

**Lesson in one line:** Some things cannot be hurried. They can only be waited for.

**Hook line (first 15 seconds):** "Tonight, over the Kingdom of Sunhill, the moon is late to rise... and Princess Baylin is about to learn that worry cannot make it come any faster."

## 12 scene beats

1. Baylin is tucked in but wide awake. Her window is dark, no moon, no stars showing yet. **Visual:** Baylin sitting up in bed, curtains open on a flat, starless navy sky.
2. Tilly the tortoise pokes her head in, noticing Baylin is still awake and restless. **Visual:** Tilly at the bedroom door, moonlight normally pooled on the floor is missing.
3. Baylin decides the moon must be lost or stuck, and that she will go and find it herself. **Visual:** Baylin pulling on a cloak over her nightgown, determined expression.
4. She and Tilly climb the hill outside the castle too quickly, and Baylin trips over a root in her hurry. **Visual:** Baylin stumbling on a moonless, shadowy hillside path, Tilly plodding steadily behind.
5. Bonayo the owl on a branch tells them the moon rises "when it is ready, not when it is called." **Visual:** Bonayo, a round, calm owl with big amber eyes, on a bare branch against the dark sky. Afrikaans: Bonayo. isiZulu: uBonayo. NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK.
6. Baylin, unconvinced, cups her hands and calls loudly up at the sky for the moon to hurry (an echo of Episode 1's "shout first" instinct). **Visual:** Baylin shouting upward, cheeks puffed, nothing happens.
7. Tilly gently suggests they stop trying to fetch it, and simply sit and wait together. **Visual:** Tilly settling onto a flat stone, patting the space beside her.
8. They sit side by side and start counting the first faint stars as they appear, one by one (a moment for the child watching to count along). **Visual:** Close-up of the sky, single stars blinking on one at a time.
9. Baylin grows doubtful, wondering aloud if the moon forgot about Sunhill altogether. **Visual:** Baylin hugging her knees, Tilly staying calm and steady beside her.
10. A faint silver glow begins at the horizon, growing slowly brighter. **Visual:** The horizon line lightening from navy to soft silver over several beats.
11. The full moon rises at last, and Baylin realises the answer was never to rush it, only to trust it and wait. **Visual:** Wide shot of the moon fully risen over Sunhill, hillside bathed in soft light.
12. Baylin and Tilly walk home calmly in the moonlight, and Baylin settles into bed, no longer restless, ready for sleep. **Visual:** Warm interior shot, moonlight through the window, Baylin's eyes closing.

**Setting:** Night sky and hillside outside the Kingdom of Sunhill (new setting from Episode 1's daytime garden/hilltop).
**Resolution type:** Patience (new from Episode 1's listening/teamwork resolution).
