# Episode 3 shot board (draft): Princess Baylin and the Quiet Star

Built from the "Visual plan per scene" table in `scripts/2026-09-30.md`, per the 2026-09-30 SHARED_UPDATES instruction. Drafts only, not for publish. Palette notes follow the script's style guide: warm peach dusk, indigo, lavender, pale gold; no pitch-black scary nights, no storms, no sharp star edges, no real child likeness.

Note: the script hedges between "a firefly friend (or a gentle moth)" throughout. This shot board picks **firefly** as the working choice, since a small warm-glow insect reads more clearly on screen next to the Quiet Star's glow than a moth would; flag for Kevin to confirm or swap before art starts (see fix suggestion 1 in `logs/critique-ep3-2026-09-30.md`).
