### [0:00-0:25] Hook

[soft market hush waking; light cloth flutter; distant basket thump]

Market morning woke the Kingdom of Sunhill. Banners fluttered. Baskets filled. Princess Baylin raced ahead with empty hands and a big plan.

"I will do one huge kindness today!"

But at the first stall, her big plan spilled. Then she saw a tiny bun pass from one paw to another, soft and free.

Come with us to a gentle market morning, where kindness learns to share in small ways.

**Visual:** Title card over soft morning stalls. Baylin racing with empty hands. Fruit colours. Calm faces. Warm dust light.

---

### [0:25-1:15] Beat 1 - Market wake

[cloth awnings rustle; soft footsteps; warm morning air]

Once upon a soft morning, when the sun was still kind and low, the market of the Kingdom of Sunhill opened its stalls like sleepy eyes.

Woven baskets. Cloth awnings in pastel pinks and peaches. Fruit the colour of morning. Animal friends arranging apples and ribbons with calm paws.

Princess Baylin bounced on her toes at the edge of the square.

"Today I will do one huge kindness before noon," she said. "The biggest kindness the market has ever seen!"

Tilly the tortoise hummed beside her. "Huge can be heavy," Tilly said. "Shall we look first?"

But Baylin's feet were already ready to race.

**Visual:** Soft morning market. Open stalls. Baylin eager. Tilly steady. Warm basket browns and fruit oranges. Distinct from Ep 5's honey-gold hillside.

---

### [1:15-2:05] Beat 2 - The big plan

[quick footsteps; basket lift; soft tumble of cloth]

Baylin raced from stall to stall with a grand plan in her head.

"I will gather many things at once for a grand share," she called. "Bread and berries and ribbons and fruit. Everyone will have everything!"

Her arms filled. Then filled again. A soft cloth slipped. A peach rolled against her elbow.

Tilly followed at her own calm pace. One careful foot. Then another.

"Baylin," Tilly called gently. "Your arms are very full."

"That means more kindness!" Baylin laughed, and she hurried on.

**Visual:** Baylin with arms too full. Soft comic hurry, never panic. Tilly smaller behind. Open gentle crowd of animal friends. No dense or scary market.

---

### [2:05-2:55] Beat 3 - Spill

[gentle tip; soft fruit roll; hush, not crash]

At the first warm stall, Baylin's big plan tipped.

Fruit rolled softly across the stones. A basket leaned. Nothing broke. No one was hurt. Only a soft tumble and a flustered little hush.

"Oh," Baylin said. Her cheeks felt warm. She was flustered, not scared. "My huge kindness... spilled."

A shy shopper stepped back. A neighbour paused. The market held its breath for a moment, the way a room holds a pause before a story continues.

Baylin looked at the rolling fruit and felt her grand plan go small.

**Visual:** Soft spill beat. Fruit rolls. Basket tips. Calm faces. No broken glass. No distress. Baylin flustered, not crying bait.

---

### [2:55-3:45] Beat 4 - Mallow notices

[soft flour dust; warm bun steam; gentle mouse voice]

From behind a flour-dusted counter, a small market mouse stepped forward with kind eyes.

This was Mallow the Market Mouse, baker of tiny warm buns and soft words.

Mallow offered Baylin a tiny bun, still warm.

"Small is enough," Mallow said. "Here. Soft and free."

Baylin took the bun with careful hands. It smelled like morning and kindness.

"I wanted to do one huge thing," Baylin whispered.

"Huge things are fine," Mallow said. "But markets are made of small gifts that fit in a paw."

**Visual:** Mallow's warm stall. Tiny buns. Flour dust. Kind eyes. Baylin receiving one bun. Soft welcoming stall light.

---

### [3:45-4:35] Beat 5 - Tilly sees

[soft tortoise shuffle; cloth shade; quiet noticing]

Tilly came up beside Baylin and pointed with a slow nod.

"Look," Tilly said. "The kindnesses you rushed past."

Baylin looked.

A neighbour had shared a shade cloth so a shy shopper could rest. Someone had turned a tipped basket upright. A smile had passed between two stalls like a little lamp.

Baylin had been too busy gathering her huge plan to see them.

"They are small," Baylin said.

"They are true," said Tilly. "We'll get there in the end. Especially when we notice."

**Visual:** Shared shade cloth. Turned basket. Soft smile between stalls. Discovery montage that stays calm. Morning pastel light.

---

### [4:35-5:25] Beat 6 - Baylin slows

[footsteps settle; basket set down; market rhythm soft]

Baylin put down the rest of her huge plan. She set her arms free.

She watched. She listened to the market's soft rhythm. Cloth flutter. Basket thump. Quiet thank-yous. A tiny bun passed from one paw to another again, soft and free.

"Oh," she whispered. "I raced to be kind. But I missed the kindness already here."

She remembered a river where a sorry needed hands that stayed. She remembered a slow hill path where friendship took its time.

"May I try small?" she asked the morning.

The morning did not answer in words. It only kept being soft.

**Visual:** Baylin still. Arms empty. Watching stalls. Soft emotional beat, no lecture. Warm dust light.

---

### [5:25-6:15] Beat 7 - Share soft and free (child pause)

[gentle underscore; soft bun unwrap; quiet invite]

[On-screen cue: "Share... soft and free"]

[3-5 second pause for the child to whisper or offer a soft smile along]

Baylin offered one small kindness of her own.

She returned a rolled fruit to its stall. She waited her turn in a short line. Then she carried a thank-you bun back to Mallow, soft and free.

"Share... soft and free," Baylin whispered.

Can you share one small kindness too? A smile. A turn. A soft word. Kindness that fits in a paw still warms a whole morning.

The market seemed friendlier at this pace. Even the banners fluttered more gently.

**Visual:** Close on Baylin's small share. Mid-story child pause with on-screen cue. Returned fruit. Thank-you bun. Calm faces.

---

### [6:15-7:05] Beat 8 - Ripple

[soft chain of kindness; fruit pass; ribbon rustle]

One small share invited another.

A neighbour passed fruit down the row. An illustrated child friend (never a real child's face) offered a ribbon for a basket handle. Someone hummed a thank-you tune.

The market felt warmer, as if the morning itself had been given a soft blanket.

Baylin laughed a quiet laugh. "It spreads," she said.

"Like jam on warm bread," Mallow said, eyes kind. "Thin, and enough for many."

Tilly smiled her slow smile. The square held more light somehow, though the sun had not moved much at all.

**Visual:** Ripple montage. Fruit pass. Ribbon for basket. Gentle animal friends. Never dense crowd. Warm fruit oranges and pinks.

---

### [7:05-7:55] Beat 9 - Together

[quiet stall-side snack; soft owl glide optional; warm hush]

Baylin, Tilly, and Mallow shared a quiet stall-side snack. Tiny buns. Soft fruit. A little rest in the shade cloth.

Above the stalls, a familiar soft shape glided once and settled on a high beam.

"Hoo-hoo."

Bonayo the owl blinked his round wise eyes, watching kindly. He did not need to speak yet. Watching was enough.

Baylin pressed her hands together for a moment, just glad.

The huge plan felt far away now. The small gifts felt near.

**Visual:** Stall-side snack. Three friends. Optional Bonayo glide and perch. Soft late-morning wash. Keep Bonayo gentle and brief.

---

### [7:55-8:45] Beat 10 - Mallow's lesson

[warm soft pad; gentle underscore]

Mallow brushed a little flour from her whiskers and looked at Baylin with steady kindness.

"Your huge plan was kind," Mallow said. "Truly. But the market is made of small gifts that add up. One soft bun. One turned basket. One smile. Together they warm a whole morning."

Baylin nodded, remembering it the way you remember a song you want to keep.

"I will try small kindnesses when I share," she said. "And notice the ones already passing by."

Tilly's eyes shone. "We'll get there in the end," she said. "Especially when we share soft and free."

**Visual:** Mallow lesson close-up. Baylin nodding. Soft emotional landing. No lecture framing. Warm stall light.

---

### [8:45-9:25] Beat 11 - Thanks and Bonayo

[soft owl call; market dusk tipping; gentle thanks]

As the morning leaned toward a warm market-dusk, Bonayo called again from his beam.

"Hoo-hoo."

"Well listened, Princess Baylin," Bonayo said, warm as old wood.

Baylin pressed her hands together in thanks.

"Thank you, Mallow. Thank you, Tilly. Thank you, soft morning market. Thank you, Bonayo."

Mallow dipped her head. Tilly hummed. Bonayo settled his feathers. The stalls held the quiet like a soft blanket.

**Visual:** Bonayo on beam (confirmed canon owl). Thanks beat. Lanterns soft, never scary. Market-dusk wash (lanterns, warm awnings).

---

### [9:25-10:00] Beat 12 - Home and soft sleep + thank-you + Ep 7 tease

[music slows; soft footsteps home; bedsheets; fade toward hush]

They walked home calmly from the market square. No race now. Only the memory of small gifts that add up, and kindness that fits in a paw.

Back in her room, Baylin climbed into bed. Her eyes felt heavy in the nicest way. Outside the window, soft market lanterns glowed for a little longer under a warm dusk sky.

"Thank you, friends!" said Baylin.

[On-screen: "Thank you, friends!" / AF "Dankie, vriende!" / ZU "Siyabonga, bangane!" - AF/ZU NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK]

Then Baylin tipped her face toward the soft dark and whispered goodnight, the way she always does when a story is done.

Who could you share one small kindness with today? Perhaps a soft word. Perhaps a turn. Perhaps something small and free.

And if you listen very carefully on another calm day, you may hear a gentle story called Princess Baylin and the Thank-You Lantern, when gratitude glows soft enough to light a whole evening.

Goodnight, little listener. Sleep well. The kind market will keep a soft watch while you dream.

[fade out]

**Visual:** Warm interior. Soft evening through the window. Distant market-lantern silhouette. Baylin's eyes closing. Thank-you ritual card. Optional soft lantern tease for a later episode. No hard sell, no end-screen style push.
