# Calm Minute Shorts: scripts for Episodes 1 to 8

One Calm Minute Short per episode, approved with its episode (Kevin, 2026-11-14). Each goes out the day after its episode and is reused off YouTube as the reel for parents.

## Format (the same for every Calm Minute)

- 30 to 45 seconds. Vertical, made for kids, drawn for the vertical frame: new art, not a crop of the episode.
- One line of story, the episode's calm cue on screen, one thing for the child to do, a five-second pause, then Bonayo's goodnight and the title card.
- Bonayo's line follows the lesson ("Well listened", "Well waited", "Well mended").
- Old-storyteller narrator and the locked hand-drawn art style. No hard sell. No distress faces.
- Description for every Short: one calm sentence about the episode, then exactly: Created from Kevin's stories, brought to life with AI.
- English only until a named native speaker has checked the Afrikaans and isiZulu lines.
- Not for publish until Kevin has watched the finished Short.

## SH01. Listen... What Do You Hear? | One Calm Minute with Princess Baylin

From Episode 1, Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song. Planned for Wednesday 25 November 2026.

- NARRATOR: The Rainbird forgot her rain song. Shouting did not bring it back. So Princess Baylin stopped, and listened.
- [far-off frogs, a soft patter, a low hum]
- [On-screen: Listen... what do you hear?]
- NARRATOR: Close your eyes and name one sound you can hear.
- [5 second pause]
- BONAYO: Well listened. Goodnight.
- CARD: Princess Baylin and the Lost Rain Song. The full bedtime story is on Princess Baylin Diaries.
