## Narration direction (production)

- Pace: calm bedtime pace, about 120 to 125 words per minute. A touch brighter than Ep2 at the start (dusk, walking), then slowing from beat 5 onward. Ep2 is the slowest episode; Ep3 sits between Ep1 and Ep2.
- Hook (first 15 seconds): say "Above the Kingdom of Sunhill, one tiny star hid behind a cloud." unhurried but by about 0:06, so a sleepy child hears the problem straight away.
- Tone: old wise man, warm and deep. Never rushed, never jokey-young, never flat-neutral.
- Pauses: about 2 seconds after "Look again. Soft and small." (beat 2) and after "For a moment, nothing moved." (beat 7); the 3 to 5 second child pause after the mid cue (beat 8); a soft 2 to 3 second hold before "Goodnight, little listener."

- Comedy beat (beat 4, lanterns): a little quicker with a smile, then drop straight to soft on "Oh. I think I was being loud with light." Funny, never frantic. The music swell is brief and gentle.
- Lost firefly (beat 6): calm and curious, never worried. No chase music.
- Volume arc: soft open on the dusk path, gentle lift in beat 4, quiet middle, small warm swell in beats 9 and 10, whisper close.
- Do not invent new dialogue beyond the approved Ep3 VO below. Bonayo has no lines in this episode.
- Dedicated EN / AF / ZU voices. Never one South African-accented English voice for all languages.

## Full voiceover script (approved VO from `scripts/2026-09-30.md`, unchanged)

Narration only below. Sound cues in [brackets]. Visual notes sit under each block. Speak slowly, warmly, like a bedtime storyteller. Pause where marked. About 1,210 words of narration. The text is copied unchanged from the approved file, including its ellipsis characters.

### [0:00-0:20] Hook

[soft dusk breeze; quiet garden hush]

Above the Kingdom of Sunhill, one tiny star hid behind a cloud. Princess Baylin looked up and almost missed it. The Quiet Star whispered, "I am too small to help anyone." Baylin smiled. "Maybe small is exactly what we need."

Come with us along a soft dusk path, under a pale indigo sky, and into a lesson that shines gently, like a light that never needed to be the brightest.

**Visual:** Title card fades in over warm peach dusk tipping into indigo. One tiny pale-gold glow peeks from a soft cloud. Calm faces.

---

### [0:20-1:10] Beat 1 - Dusk settles

[soft footsteps on a garden path; distant hush of evening insects, very gentle]

Once upon a soft evening, when the day was folding itself away, Princess Baylin and Tilly the tortoise walked the garden path outside the palace.

The big bright stars were already shining. They looked proud and far away, like lanterns hung high for ships and travellers.

But Baylin's eyes caught something else. Or almost caught it. A tiny light, smaller than a pinprick of gold, was hiding behind a cotton-soft cloud.

"Do you see that?" Baylin asked.

Tilly blinked slowly. "I see a sky with many lights. And one that is trying not to be seen."

**Visual:** Garden path at soft dusk. Warm peach sky tipping to indigo. Big stars bright. One tiny shy glow half-hidden by a soft cloud. Baylin and Tilly walking calmly.

---

### [1:10-2:00] Beat 2 - Look again

[soft tortoise shuffle pause; grass whisper]

Baylin almost walked past. The big stars were easier to look at. They shone without asking.

Tilly paused on the path and tipped her head toward the cloud.

"Look again," said Tilly softly. "Soft and small."

Baylin stopped. She looked again, carefully this time, the way you look for a firefly when the night is first arriving.

There it was. A shy little glow, blinking once, then hiding.

"Hello, little light," Baylin whispered. "We nearly missed you."

The tiny star peeked out a little more, as if being noticed felt both scary and kind.

**Visual:** Close on the cloud edge. Tiny star peeks. Baylin crouches or tips her face up. Tilly still and calm. Soft lavender and pale gold accents.

---

### [2:00-2:50] Beat 3 - Too small

[very soft shy whisper; barely-there twinkle]

Behind the soft cloud, a small voice spoke. It was not loud. It was the sound of someone who has practised being quiet.

"I am too small," whispered the Quiet Star. "The bright stars guide ships across the sea. I only blink. Who would follow a blink?"

Baylin's face grew gentle. She remembered how hard it was, once, to wait for a moon that would not hurry. She remembered how listening had brought a rain song home.

"You are not nothing," said Baylin. "You are a light. Even a small one."

The Quiet Star hid a little deeper. "Small lights do not matter."

**Visual:** Soft cloud with tiny pale-gold star. Baylin looking up kindly. No sharp edges. No distress faces. Quiet Star shy, not sad-bait.

---

### [2:50-3:40] Beat 4 - Lanterns too loud

[soft dance steps; lantern swish; then hush as star retreats]

Baylin wanted to help. Helping felt like doing something big and bright.

She danced a little on the path. She waved two soft garden lanterns, one in each hand, hoping the Quiet Star would feel cheered.

"See?" she called. "We can make lots of light together!"

[music swells briefly, then softens]

But the lanterns were too much. Too much glow. Too much movement. The Quiet Star slipped further behind the cloud, until even the tiny peek was gone.

Baylin lowered the lanterns. Her cheeks felt warm.

"Oh," she said. "I think I was being loud with light."

**Visual:** Baylin dancing and waving lanterns. Soft comic beat, never distress. Cloud closes over the Quiet Star. Tilly watching calmly.

---

### [3:40-4:30] Beat 5 - Soft glow close by

[slow tortoise shuffle closer; soft breeze moves grass]

Tilly stepped closer, one careful foot after another.

"Big lights are lovely," said Tilly. "Lanterns and bright stars have their place. But sometimes a friend does not need a parade. Sometimes a friend needs a soft glow close by."

Baylin sat the lanterns down on the path. The dusk felt quieter again.

"I wanted the Quiet Star to feel brave," Baylin said.

"Bravery can be quiet," said Tilly. "Like sitting. Like looking again. Like asking softly."

Baylin nodded. The hurry in her hands began to settle.

**Visual:** Lanterns set down. Tilly close to Baylin. Cloud still closed. Soft indigo and peach. Calm two-shot.

---

### [4:30-5:20] Beat 6 - Lost in the grass

[gentle moth or firefly wing flutter; tall grass rustle; no chase music]

Then a tiny problem arrived, as tiny problems sometimes do at bedtime.

A firefly friend (or a gentle moth, soft as a thumb of dusk) had lost the path home through the tall grass. The bright stars were beautiful, but they were too high and too far to mark the little twists between the stems.

[soft confused flutter, still calm]

Baylin watched the small wings zigzag, then pause, then zigzag again.

"The big stars cannot help from up there," she whispered. "Someone needs a light that is close."

Tilly's eyes were kind. "Someone soft and small, perhaps."

**Visual:** Tall soft grass. Tiny firefly or moth, calm and a little lost, never frantic. Big stars high above. Baylin and Tilly noticing. No chase, no fear faces.

---

### [5:20-6:00] Beat 7 - One soft blink

[pause; soft breeze; Baylin settles on the path]

Baylin sat still on the garden path beside Tilly. She did not wave the lanterns. She did not dance. She looked at the cloud the way you look at a shy friend who needs room.

"Quiet Star," she said softly. "We do not need a parade. We need one soft blink. Just one. A firefly friend has lost the path through the grass. Your light is close enough to help."

For a moment, nothing moved. The big stars stayed bright and far. The cloud stayed soft.

Then, from behind the cotton edge, a pale gold tip of light appeared.

**Visual:** Baylin and Tilly sitting still. Cloud edge. Tiny pale-gold tip peeks. Soft pause. Faces calm and hopeful, not worried.

---

### [6:00-6:50] Beat 8 - Shine soft and small (child pause)

[very soft twinkle; one gentle blink]

[On-screen cue: "Shine… soft and small"]

[3-5 second pause for the child to whisper or shine along]

The Quiet Star peeked all the way out. Not bright like a ship-star. Soft. Small. Exactly enough.

"Shine… soft and small," Baylin whispered.

Can you shine with the Quiet Star too? Soft. Small. One gentle blink. You do not need to be the brightest light in the room to be a kind one.

The Quiet Star blinked once. Then again. Each blink was a little braver than the last, and still quiet.

**Visual:** Close on Quiet Star peeking from cloud. Mid-story child pause with on-screen cue. Optional soft glow pulse. Pale gold on indigo. No flashy effects.

---

### [6:50-7:40] Beat 9 - Path of tiny lights

[soft sequential twinkles along the grass; gentle wing flutter finding home]

Tiny blinks began to mark the grass path. One soft glow here. Another a little further on. Like stepping stones made of kindness.

The firefly (or gentle moth) followed them, wing by careful wing. No chase. No fear. Only a soft path home through the tall grass.

Baylin held her breath, then let it out slowly.

"You did it," she whispered to the Quiet Star. "You guided a friend."

The last blink rested near a safe little hollow in the grass. The tiny wings settled. Home.

**Visual:** Sequence of soft pale-gold blinks along a grass path. Firefly or moth follows calmly. Wide soft dusk shot. Never scary tall grass. Never chase framing.

---

### [7:40-8:30] Beat 10 - Small can still guide

[warm soft pad; night colours gentle]

The Quiet Star glowed a little steadier now. Still small. Still quiet. But no longer hiding.

"My light was enough," said the Quiet Star, surprised and soft.

Baylin smiled the way she smiles when a hard thing turns gentle.

"Small can still guide," she said. "You do not need to be the brightest to matter. Quiet courage counts."

Tilly nodded, slow as dusk itself. "The brightest star is not always the one a friend needs."

Baylin looked up at the big stars, then at the Quiet Star, and felt glad for both.

**Visual:** Quiet Star steady and soft outside the cloud. Baylin and Tilly looking up. Warm emotional beat, no tears bait. Peach, indigo, pale gold.

---

### [8:30-9:10] Beat 11 - Thanks and a wink

[soft breeze; optional single gentle moon wink chime]

Baylin stood and pressed her hands together in thanks.

"Thank you, Quiet Star. Thank you, Tilly."

The Quiet Star blinked once, shy and proud at the same time.

High above, the Sleepy Moon may have winked once. Just once. A friendly cameo from another quiet night, not a new adventure. Then the sky stayed soft and still.

Baylin felt the garden path under her feet, and the kindness of a light that chose to shine small on purpose.

**Visual:** Baylin thanking. Quiet Star soft blink. Optional Sleepy Moon wink in the corner of the sky (cameo only). Calm wide shot of dusk garden.

---

### [9:10-10:00] Beat 12 - Home and soft sleep + thank-you + Ep 4 tease

[music slows; soft footsteps home; bedsheets; fade toward hush]

They walked home calmly under the soft dusk sky. No hurry now. No loud lanterns. Only the memory of a path marked by tiny lights.

Back in her room, Baylin climbed into bed. Her eyes felt heavy in the nicest way. Outside the window, one small star still glowed, soft and small and enough.

"Thank you, friends!" said Baylin.

[On-screen: "Thank you, friends!" / AF "Dankie, vriende!" / ZU "Siyabonga, bangane!" - AF/ZU NEEDS NATIVE-SPEAKER CHECK]

Then Baylin tipped her face toward the sky at her window and whispered goodnight, the way she always does when a story is done.

What small kindness can you share today? Perhaps a soft word. Perhaps waiting your turn. Small lights still guide friends.

And if you listen very carefully on another quiet night, you may hear the river that whispered, or a gentle story about saying sorry, when Princess Baylin learns another soft kind of courage.

Goodnight, little listener. Sleep well. The Quiet Star will keep a soft watch while you dream.

[fade out]

**Visual:** Warm interior. Soft dusk-to-night through the window. Tiny star glow. Baylin's eyes closing. Thank-you ritual card. Optional soft river silhouette or sorry-story silhouette tease for a later episode. No hard sell, no end-screen style push.
