# Critique: Episode 3 script (`scripts/2026-09-30.md`)

Reviewed for bedtime pacing, narration word count, and kid-safety, per the 2026-09-30 SHARED_UPDATES instruction.

## Word count
Counted the "Full voiceover script" section only (narration and dialogue lines, excluding bracketed sound cues, on-screen cue markers, and **Visual:** notes): **about 1,050-1,100 words** (manual count by beat; a few short beats like 5, 6 and 9 run closer to 65-75 words each, the hook and beat 12 run longer at roughly 75-140 words). That sits inside the script's own 1,000-1,250 target. At a slow bedtime pace (110-130 words per minute) that is roughly 8.5-9.5 minutes of narration, which fits the stated 8-10 minute runtime once the beat 8 child pause and visual holds are added. No change needed here, just confirming the target was hit.

## Kid-safety
No distress-bait found. The "lost" firefly/moth in beat 6 is explicitly calm ("no chase, no fear faces" in the visual note), the Quiet Star's self-doubt is resolved without tears, and Baylin's lantern mistake in beat 4 is played as a gentle comic beat rather than a failure. Good as is.

## Fix suggestions (numbered)

1. **Lock the firefly-or-moth choice before the shot board.** Beats 6, 9 and the Short script all hedge with "a firefly friend (or a gentle moth...)". That's fine for a story draft, but the shot board and VO recording both need one specific creature, since the visuals and any sound cue differ (wingbeat, size, colour). Pick one now so the shot board task doesn't have to guess.

2. **Firm up the Sleepy Moon cameo line.** Beat 11 has "High above, the Sleepy Moon may have winked once." The hedge ("may have") reads oddly out loud for a narrator stating what happened in the story. Suggest: "High above, the Sleepy Moon winked once. Just once." to match the confident, calm narrator voice used everywhere else.

3. **Trim the double Ep 4 tease.** The closing line offers two competing hooks in one breath: "you may hear the river that whispered, or a gentle story about saying sorry." Two options diluted into one sentence is a weaker hook than one clear tease. This is already flagged as `[KEVIN]` decision flag 11 ("Approve soft tease of later Episode 4 candidates"); recommend Kevin pick one, not both, once decided.

4. **Balance the repeated "too small" self-talk.** The Quiet Star says some version of "I am too small" / "small lights do not matter" three times (hook, beat 3, beat 3 again) before Baylin's reassurance lands in beat 7-8. For the younger end of the 4-8 range, consider moving one reassuring line earlier (even a short one from Tilly) so the self-doubt doesn't sit unanswered for over two minutes of runtime.

5. **Tighten beat 4's "too much light" beat.** Baylin's dancing-with-lanterns bit is a nice gentle-mistake beat, but "too much glow, too much movement" plus the star "hiding until even the tiny peek was gone" risks reading as Baylin causing the Quiet Star mild distress. The **Visual** note already says "soft comic beat, never distress," which is the right instinct; suggest softening the narration itself slightly, e.g. drop "until even the tiny peek was gone" so the star recedes rather than fully vanishes.

6. **Chapter timestamps run right to the 10:00 edge.** The 12 chapter markers plus the 9:10-10:00 closing beat land exactly at the upper end of the stated "8 to 10 minute" runtime, with no buffer. If the VO reads slightly slower than the 110-130 wpm estimate (common for a warm, slow "old wise man" narrator per the Kevin 2026-09-30 voice decision), the episode could run past 10 minutes. Consider trimming a line or two from the longer beats (hook, beat 12) to build in a margin.

7. **Consider one more interactive pause.** Only beat 8 ("Shine... soft and small") has an explicit on-screen child-participation cue. A second short pause around beat 9 (for example, "can you count the tiny lights too?") would help hold attention through the back half, matching the same suggestion made for Episode 2's critique.

## Overall
Script is close to production-ready on pacing, word count and safety grounds. The suggestions above are polish, not blockers. Kevin's approval decisions (placeholders, narrator, art style, AI disclosure, Ep 4 tease) remain the actual gate, per the `[KEVIN] decision flags` at the bottom of `scripts/2026-09-30.md`.
