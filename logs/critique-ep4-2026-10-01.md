# Critique: Episode 4 script (scripts/2026-10-01.md), 2026-10-01

Scope: pacing, word count, kid-safety. Concrete edits only, no rewrite performed.

**Word count:** Full narration (excluding sound cues and visual notes) is about 1,131 words, inside the 1,000-1,250 target. No whole-script trim needed.

1. **Hook is paced too fast.** About 74 words are packed into the 0:00-0:20 slot (~220 words per minute), roughly double the calm pace used everywhere else in the script (Beats 1-11 average ~100 wpm). Either trim the hook to about 35-40 words or extend its slot to about 35-40 seconds and shift the chapter markers that follow.
2. **Closing beat (Beat 12, "Home and soft sleep") is paced too fast.** About 143 words are packed into the final 9:20-10:00 slot (~215 wpm), the densest segment in the episode, right where the pace should be slowest for a sleepy wind-down. Recommend splitting it into two chapter markers (for example "Home and Soft Sleep" then a separate "Goodnight") or adding 30-40 seconds to the back end so the outro is not rushed.
3. **Mid-episode beats have slack that could offset the above.** Beat 2 (~92 wpm) and Beat 10 (~90 wpm) are the most comfortably paced; trimming 5-10 words from two or three of the roomier beats and adding that time to the Hook and Beat 12 slots would fix 1 and 2 without changing total runtime.
4. **Child-pause instruction does not match the on-screen mantra.** Beat 7's visual note asks the child to "place a soft 'sorry' along," but the on-screen chant and the episode's repeated mantra is "Mend... soft and true," not "sorry." Align the pause instruction with the mantra that is actually shown so a child watching is not cued to say a word that never appears on screen.
5. **Title option 2 may conflict with a reserved future title.** "Princess Baylin Says Sorry" (title option 2) is the same title the project flagged on 2026-09-27 as a strong candidate for a possible future stand-alone episode. Using it here as an alt title for Episode 4 risks a naming clash later. Suggest dropping option 2 and keeping "River That Whispered" and "The Sorry That Learned to Slow Down" as the two choices, or confirm with Kevin that "Says Sorry" is now considered used by Episode 4.
6. **Kid-safety: no issues found.** The splash/"accident" moment is consistently de-escalated in every relevant visual note ("soft scatter, never violent," "no danger framing," "no drowning risk framing," "Pip sad not angry"). No changes needed here; keep this pattern for future episodes.
7. **Runtime estimate is at the edge of its stated range.** The header says "8 to 10 minutes," and the chapter markers run to 10:00 exactly. Once 1-3 are applied the runtime may extend slightly past 10:00; update the header estimate to match the final cut rather than leaving it at the upper bound.
