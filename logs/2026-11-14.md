## Why Episodes 5 and 6 were changed more than the critiques asked

The three October drafts each opened with Baylin racing ahead and closed with her slowing down. YouTube's monetisation policy excludes content that puts characters through the same situation and outcome on a repeated template, and it judges the whole channel. Changing only the first 30 seconds would have left "Race you to the meadow!" in Episode 5, one beat after "Race you to the willow!" in Episode 4. So:

- Episode 4 keeps the race. It causes the accident, and the lesson is making amends.
- Episode 5: Baylin's motive is now a kind one that goes wrong. She hurries ahead to lay the picnic out for Tilly. Lines changed in Beats 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9. The lesson, the cue and the structure are unchanged.
- Episode 6: Baylin's mistake is a plan that is too big, not feet that are too fast. Lines changed in Beats 1, 2, 5, 6 and 7.
