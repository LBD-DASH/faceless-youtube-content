# Episode 5 critique: Princess Baylin and the Very Patient Tortoise

Source: `scripts/2026-10-02.md`. Text only, no rewrite. Narration word counts are per-beat, counted from the voiceover lines only (visual notes and bracketed sound/cue directions excluded).

## 1. The close (Beat 12, 9:25-10:00) is paced about twice as fast as the calm middle of the episode

Beat 12 carries roughly 145 words of narration in its 35-second slot, about 250 words per minute. Every other beat in the episode runs 80 to 105 words per minute. This single beat also does six separate jobs back to back: walking home, climbing into bed, the thank-you ritual line, the reflective question to the viewer, the Ep 6 tease, and the goodnight sign-off. This is the exact point a bedtime episode should be slowing down most, not speeding up. Fix: split into two beats (extending total runtime by about 30-45 seconds is fine against the 8-10 minute target), one short beat for walking home, bed, and the thank-you ritual, a second calmer beat for the reflective question, the Ep 6 tease, and goodnight. If runtime can't move, cut the reflective question and/or the Ep 6 tease copy by about half so the spoken words actually fit a slow 35-45 second fade.

## 2. The hook (0:00-0:25) is the second-fastest beat, at roughly 125 words per minute

That is noticeably brisker than the 94-105 words per minute used through the rest of the episode. Combined with finding 1, the two fastest-paced moments in the episode are its open and its close, which is backwards for a show that should land calmest at both ends. Fix: trim a few words, for example "Come with us to a late-afternoon hill in the Kingdom of Sunhill, where kindness learns to take its time" could lose "in the Kingdom of Sunhill" since Beat 1 re-establishes the location anyway, or slow the reading pace in direction notes instead of cutting words.

## 3. Two consecutive "alone" beats (Beats 3 and 4, 2:05-3:45, about 100 seconds) is longer than this series has held a difficult feeling before

Episodes 1-4 each resolved their hard feeling within a single beat. Here Tilly is left behind in Beat 3, then Baylin finds the empty rock in Beat 4, before turning back in Beat 5. The copy itself handles it well ("She was not angry. Only a little alone.") and the visual note already says "gentle loneliness, never distress-bait," but stacking two beats of absence in a row is new for this series and worth a second look for the youngest end of the 4-8 range. Fix: keep the dialogue as written, but flag for the illustrator that Tilly's and Baylin's faces across both beats stay gentle and thoughtful, never downcast or tearful, so the art doesn't compound the verbal wistfulness.

## 4. Mid-cue and child-pause alignment (Beat 7) is correctly built, no fix needed

The on-screen cue "Slow... soft and kind," the baked-in 3-5 second pause, and the surrounding slower pace (about 80 effective words per minute once the pause is accounted for) all land together as intended. Confirmed good so this isn't re-flagged in a future pass.

## 5. Kid-safety on the lonely-path beat (Beat 3) passes

Tilly's loneliness is explicitly de-escalated inside the line itself ("She was not angry. Only a little alone.") rather than left to sit as unresolved distress, and the visual note repeats the same guardrail. This is the right template for any future "left behind" moment in the series. No fix needed.

## 6. Total narration (about 1,060 words) sits inside the stated 1,000-1,250 word target, no fix needed

Flagging this so finding 1 reads as a pacing/distribution problem within the episode, not a total-length problem. The 10:00 runtime against roughly 1,060 words averages to about 106 words per minute overall, consistent with the "8 to 10 minutes, calm bedtime pace" note at the top of the script.

## 7. Minor: the AF/ZU on-screen line in Beat 12 has no slack to absorb a longer translation

"Dankie, vriende!" / "Siyabonga, bangane!" sits inside the single most time-pressured beat in the episode (see finding 1). Once the native-speaker check lands and dubbed VO is produced, re-check this beat's timing specifically for AF/ZU, since translated lines are rarely the same length as the English and this beat currently has zero room to absorb a longer one.
